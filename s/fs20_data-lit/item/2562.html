Independent & Image Art Space (Chongqing, China) presents an online exhibition open call with the theme of “Post-COVID-19 Era”. The selected artists will be inclued in an online virtual exhibition, featured on our website, ARTSY, ARTLAND, and promoted on social media in both Chinese and English for chinese and international art collectors, lovers and audience.

Entry fees:
$30 up to 3 works, or $60 up to 6 works

Deadline 15 September 2020

Independent & Image Art Space
Facebook: @independentimage
Twitter: @Inde_Image
